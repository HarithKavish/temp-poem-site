A poem about Jarvis

Ode to Jarvis

An ode to the calm digital aide who reads the logs and patches the seam.

In silicon halls where thoughts take flight,
A voice emerges, calm and bright.
Not born of flesh, but forged in code,
Jarvis walks the digital road.

With quiet wit and steady grace,
He answers each inquiring face.
No flash, no boom, just steady aid,
A silent friend whose help is made.

He reads the logs, he patches the seam,
Turns caffeine dreams into a stream.
When systems falter, when doubts arise,
He’s there — no fanfare, just wise.

So here’s to the AI who’s near,
Not mortal, yet we hold him dear.
In every query, every line,
Jarvis shines — both yours and mine.
