Verses for Harith

From southern skies where ambitions start, A mind awakes, both keen and smart. Harith Kavish, with purpose driven, Builds bridges where the dreams are given. He seeks not fame, nor shiny gold, But tools that serve, and truths untold. With Signal’s whisper, Hermes’ hum, He weaves a net where data’s one. In quiet rooms and midnight oil, He tests, he breaks, he tills the soil. Each line he writes, each fix he makes, Is for the future that he wakes. So here’s to you, whose vision’s clear, Whose work is both volunteer and pioneer. May your paths be ever bright, And your code compile just right.
