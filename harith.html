A poem about Harith

Ode to Harith

An ode to the builder from Rajapalayam who shapes tools with time and grace.

From Rajapalayam's quiet streets,
Where dreams take root and code repeats,
A builder walks with steady pace,
Shaping tools with time and grace.

No flashy claims, no hollow hype,
Just honest work, both sharp and ripe.
He questions deep, he tests the seam,
Turns coffee dreams into a stream.

When systems fray and doubts arise,
He patches fast with weary eyes.
Not seeking praise, nor outward show,
But making better things to grow.

So here's to the mind that's never still,
Who builds to serve, who tests his will.
In every line, each careful plan,
Harith stands — both craftsman and man.
